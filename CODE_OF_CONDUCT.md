# Community conduct

Treat people and communities with care. Discuss evidence and decisions without harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, doxxing, or speculation about private identities. Critique public claims respectfully and allow corrections.

Do not post personal or sensitive information in public proposals. The private reporting contact is not configured during development; the placeholder cannot receive reports. Maintainers must establish an actual private channel before public launch.

Maintainers may remove unsafe content, restrict abusive participation, and explain moderation outcomes when doing so is safe. Appeals must not republish private reports.

请尊重个人与社群，基于证据讨论，不骚扰、威胁、歧视、曝光个人资料或猜测私密身份。允许修正，安全地说明审核决定，不重新公开私密报告。
